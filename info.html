Tour Information

Everything you need to know before you travel

What to Carry

Spiti is a high-altitude Himalayan destination. We strongly recommend carrying the following:

  • Warm layers and windproof jackets (temperatures drop drastically at night).
  • Comfortable trekking or walking shoes.
  • Sunglasses and high SPF Sunscreen.
  • Personal medicines (including motion sickness and altitude medication).
  • Reusable water bottle and a power bank.

Accommodation & Meals (MAP Plan)

Our packages include accommodation with a Modified American Plan (MAP). This means your Breakfast, Dinner, and Accommodation are all included in the package cost. Lunch can be offered separately or added upon request.

Transportation

We provide planned transportation for the complete Manali–Spiti–Manali journey. Depending on your group size, we arrange suitable vehicles (like Tempo Travellers or robust SUVs). Our focus is always on Safety, Comfort, and Reliable Service.

Optional Experiences

To make your journey even more memorable, we offer add-ons like Stargazing, Bonfire evenings, Photography guides, and Traditional Spitian food experiences (subject to weather and availability).
