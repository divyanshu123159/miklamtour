YOUR BASE IN SPITI

Tour Packages & Itineraries

One comfortable base at Miklam Homestay, Rangrik — day trips out, and back every evening

Every package is built around Miklam Homestay in Rangrik — instead of packing and unpacking at a new town each night, you head out for day trips and return to the same room every evening. Transport, accommodation at the homestay, and breakfast & dinner (MAP plan) are included. Your trip starts and ends with a pickup and drop-off in Manali.

Spiti Classic Expedition

The essential Spiti introduction — one day out to Kaza's high-altitude villages, based the whole time at Miklam Homestay in Rangrik.

3 Days / 2 Nights ₹15,000 / person
Day 1

Manali → Miklam Homestay, Rangrik

~200 KM 3,800 M

Depart Manali early and cross the Atal Tunnel and Kunzum Pass, with a stop at the Chandratal viewpoint en route. Arrive at Miklam Homestay by evening, check in, and settle in for the next two nights.

Day 2

Kaza Local Circuit (day trip)

~65 KM LOOP UP TO 4,587 M

A full day out from the homestay: Key Monastery, Kibber village, the dramatic Chicham Bridge, and the high-altitude villages of Langza, Komic, and Hikkim — home to the world's highest post office. Back at Miklam Homestay by evening.

Day 3

Miklam Homestay → Manali

~200 KM 2,050 M

Check out and begin the drive back over Kunzum Pass and the Atal Tunnel. Dropped off in Manali by evening — trip concludes.

Himalayan Monastic Trail

Starts at Miklam Homestay like the other packages, then continues on into Kinnaur — Nako, Kalpa, and Sarahan — for the deepest monastery trail we run. Accommodation is included throughout; once you're on the Kinnaur leg, exact rooms are confirmed as the trip progresses.

7 Days / 6 Nights ₹22,000 / person
Day 1

Manali → Miklam Homestay, Rangrik

~200 KM 3,800 M

Cross the Atal Tunnel and Kunzum Pass, with a stop at the Chandratal viewpoint. Check in at Miklam Homestay for two nights.

Day 2

Kaza Local Circuit (day trip)

~65 KM LOOP UP TO 4,587 M

Key Monastery, Kibber, Chicham Bridge, Langza, Komic, and Hikkim's post office. Back at the homestay by evening.

Day 3

Miklam Homestay → Tabo (via Dhankar)

~50 KM 3,280 M

Check out of Miklam Homestay and visit the cliff-top Dhankar Monastery and its glacial lake on the way to Tabo — home to a 1,000-year-old monastery often called the "Ajanta of the Himalayas." Room provided in the Tabo area for the night.

Day 4

Tabo → Nako (via Kinnaur)

~85 KM 3,662 M

Leave the Spiti valley and enter Kinnaur, visiting Nako's monastery and its sacred lake. Room provided in Nako for the night.

Day 5

Nako → Kalpa (via Reckong Peo)

~55 KM 2,960 M

Descend toward Kalpa for uninterrupted views of the Kinnaur Kailash range. Room provided in Kalpa for the night.

Day 6

Kalpa → Sarahan

~130 KM 2,165 M

Continue down the valley to Sarahan, home of the Bhimakali Temple. Room provided in Sarahan for the night.

Day 7

Sarahan → Manali (via Shimla)

~220 KM 2,050 M

A long final drive back to Manali via Shimla, closing the full Manali–Spiti–Kinnaur–Manali loop. Dropped off in Manali by evening — trip concludes.
