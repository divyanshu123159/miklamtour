Our policies and safety guidelines

Important Travel Information

Spiti Valley is a remote, high-altitude Himalayan destination. Road conditions, snowfall, rainfall, landslides, local restrictions, and weather can sometimes affect the itinerary.

Access to locations like Chandratal Lake and Kunzum Top is strictly subject to road and weather conditions. Our priority is always YOUR SAFETY FIRST. For safety reasons, the itinerary may be modified by our drivers or guides when required without prior notice.

Booking & Payment Policy

  • A standard advance payment is required to confirm your booking and lock in your seats/hotel rooms.
  • Package prices can be customized according to the number of guests, vehicle type, and room category.

Cancellation Policy

In the event of road closures due to severe weather (e.g., Atal Tunnel or Kunzum Pass blockages), MIKLAM Tours will assist in rearranging the itinerary or offering a partial refund based on the terms established at the time of booking, minus non-refundable hotel and permit costs.
